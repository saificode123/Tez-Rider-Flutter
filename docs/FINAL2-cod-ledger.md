# Stage2 COD cash and refund records

Approved A remains COD-only. Migration `202610230008_final2_cod_ledger.sql` adds an append-only, private cash-event ledger and trusted `final2_record_cash` / `final2_order_payment` APIs. A delivered order stays unpaid until the assigned approved Rider or Admin explicitly acknowledges the exact canonical cash amount and a receipt reference. Recording an acknowledgement does not transfer funds. Refund eligibility stays pending until an approved Admin records an actual manual return; an unsuccessful attempt records zero returned cash and remains retryable. Existing payment rows, pending-action history and accepted FR26 negative guards are preserved.

The server locks canonical relationships, validates live role/ownership, exact finite amounts, explicit confirmation and eligible business state, and atomically persists the result, actor audit and owned inbox notice. Request UUID replay and unique result indexes prevent duplicate collections/refunds; a fresh request cannot overwrite an existing receipt. Direct table writes and direct REST RPC budget bypass remain denied. Vendor projections mask whole-parent amounts and private receipt references. Current cash records are separate from Rider wages; no compensation formula is inferred.

A retry records the result; users are told not to repeat a money transfer.
