Migration010 extends the accepted FR32 outbox/private invoice pipeline to committed `cod_cash_events` collections. The original pending payment and refund-action history remain unchanged. The invoice snapshot binds the immutable checkout items/cents to the canonical cash-event ID, actor role and acknowledgement time. UUID replay produces one invoice and one outbox intent. No fee, new provider, cash transfer or automatic refund is introduced.

Incomplete legacy checkout sources create `SOURCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` metadata instead of invented invoice items or PDF readiness. Existing invoice snapshots are preserved. Only previously committed collections without invoice metadata are caught up. Private helpers remain inaccessible to authenticated/service callers. The new invoice/outbox result is atomic with the cash event; an injected outbox failure rolls back both.
